# Idle worker

A lead agent sends a tracked request to a worker. The worker's pane renders working, goes idle a few seconds later and does not reply. Namedrop settles the request attempt by observation 30 s after it was created, with no disposition. The assignment is still open and still awaits the worker, so Doctor must not flag the attempt. The worker replies later through the same envelope, the lead resolves `completed`, and Doctor stays clean. When the lead cancels such a coordination while the request is still unanswered, Doctor must flag the attempt as `ND-DELIVERY-003`.

## Sub-features

- `idle-worker-settle` settles an acknowledged tracked request whose worker is idle 30 s after `created_at_ms`, with no disposition.
- `idle-worker-doctor-open` shows no `ND-DELIVERY-003` for that attempt while its coordination is open and the assignment awaits the worker.
- `idle-worker-late-reply` accepts the worker's `reply` on the settled attempt, records disposition `reply`, and keeps Doctor clean through the lead's `completed` resolve.
- `idle-worker-cancel-flag` raises `ND-DELIVERY-003` with the attempt id as `diagnostic.source_id` once the lead resolves the coordination `cancelled` with the request still unanswered.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Lead a tracked handoff from a registered agent session. Run `handoff begin`, then `send` with the `coordination_id` to `@worker`.
- The worker reads the request, works for a few seconds, ends its turn idle without running the bound `reply` or `complete --no-reply`, for example because it hit a tool error or ran out of turn. Nothing else happens on its pane for over 30 s.
- Before the fix, `namedrop doctor --json` showed `ND-DELIVERY-003` "An agent response ended without a clear result" with the worker attempt id in `diagnostic.source_id` about 30 s after the request was sent, although the lead's tracked assignment was still open and awaiting the worker. When the worker eventually replied, the reply was accepted and the warning cleared itself.
- The wanted behavior keeps Doctor silent about that attempt while the assignment awaits the worker. The warning appears only when that work ends without a reply or no-reply from the worker, for example when the lead resolves the coordination `cancelled` while the request is unanswered.

## Driving it with control-namedrop

Preconditions: the same as [stuck wake](stuck-wake.md). The helper links the candidate as a disposable plugin, activates it with `scripts/setup.sh activate --json`, compiles the stdin-to-stdout `codex` simulator, runs it in the worker pane and in a third pane for the lead, registers both sessions through one working-then-idle flip each, and requires Doctor's `available_agents` to be 2.

- Run `.agents/skills/verify/scripts/control-namedrop.py --feature idle-worker --binary <candidate>`.
- Positive case. The lead begins a tracked handoff and sends `@worker Compute the synthetic checksum and reply with it.` The worker's request attempt is acknowledged on dispatch. Its `created_at_ms` is T0. The worker renders working as soon as the envelope arrives, then renders idle at T0 + 5 s. The helper requires Herdr's idle observation within 25 s of T0, which keeps it inside the 30 s grace. A later observation stops the run as inconclusive instead of failing it.
- The helper polls the attempt's state and disposition every 0.5 s for up to 50 s, with no status change and no Namedrop operation in that window. It requires the attempt to reach `settled` with no disposition. On this host that happened between T0 + 30.1 s and T0 + 30.5 s across three runs. A full run takes about 1.5 min; the stop at commit 0236bf1 comes about 42 s in. Doctor then runs from the operator pane as `doctor_after_settle`, and the recipe requires no issue with code `ND-DELIVERY-003` in that read. At commit 0236bf1 this is where the run stops, with `idle worker: doctor_after_settle shows no ND-DELIVERY-003; observed 1 ND-DELIVERY-003 issue(s) with source_id ['<attempt>']: [...]` quoting the issue.
- The same drain that settled the attempt nudges the idle lead, because the assignment is pending and nothing is running. The helper receives the nudge for this coordination and completes it with its bound `complete.no-reply`.
- At least 10 s after the settle, the worker runs its original envelope's bound `reply` with `work_outcome` `completed`. The reply must exit 0 and the attempt must then carry disposition `reply`. Doctor runs as `doctor_after_reply` and must show no `ND-DELIVERY-003`. That read still shows `ND-COORDINATION-001` for this coordination, because the lead completed the first nudge without a work decision. It clears once the resolve below settles its report.
- The lead receives the reply envelope and completes it with its bound `complete.no-reply`. That completion drains, and the reply advanced the coordination's revision, so a second nudge arrives. Its `handoff.resolve` operation with outcome `completed` is bound because every assignment is now completed, and it prefills `reconciled_assignments` at the current revision. The helper runs it with a one-line report, completes the nudge, receives the report envelope and completes it. Doctor runs as `doctor_after_resolve` and must show no `ND-DELIVERY-003` and no `ND-COORDINATION-001` naming this coordination.
- Negative case, same fixture. The lead begins a second handoff and sends `@worker Compute the second synthetic checksum and reply with it.` The worker renders working, then idle at T0 + 5 s, and never replies. The attempt settles without a disposition as before. Doctor runs as `doctor_after_second_settle` and must show no `ND-DELIVERY-003`. The nudge arrives and the helper completes it.
- No bound `handoff.resolve` offers the `cancelled` outcome, so the lead resolves this coordination `cancelled` through the CLI from its own pane with explicit context, which the owner session may do. The helper then polls Doctor once a second for up to 20 s until an issue with code `ND-DELIVERY-003` whose `diagnostic.source_id` is the second worker attempt id appears, records that read as `doctor_after_cancel`, and requires it. It also records whether `ND-COORDINATION-001` names the second coordination, which is expected after a cancel and is not required. It completes the report envelope that the cancel queued for the lead.
- Every assertion after setup is a `require` whose message names the requirement and the observed value. `summary.json` records `outcome` as `passed`, `inconclusive`, or the failing requirement. A run that fails before the first worker attempt exists, or on a wait timeout, has no `outcome`.
- `idle-worker.json` is saved on every stop. It keeps panes, sessions, simulator PIDs and registrations, then one record per case under `positive` and `negative`: the coordination id, the assignment, the worker attempt id, the request envelope, T0, the working and idle observations with the idle hook's start, every poll, the settle time and its offset from T0, the plugin log rows that started between the idle hook and the settle as `events_in_window`, every Doctor read under the names above, the nudge envelopes, the reply, the resolve or cancel responses and the report envelopes. `lead-envelopes.json` and `worker-envelopes.json` keep every envelope each simulator received. `state.sqlite3` is backed up after any stop other than `passed` and after a pass.

## Gotchas

- Only an acknowledged attempt settles by observation. A submitted attempt on an idle pane past the grace is held instead and shows as `ND-DELIVERY-002`. Agent panes are acknowledged on dispatch, so the worker's request settles.
- The grace is 30 s from the attempt's `created_at_ms`, not from the idle observation. The worker idles at T0 + 5 s so the status worker's grace timer, not a hook, performs the settle.
- A single-recipient tracked reply binds only `reply`, `complete.no-reply` and the context operations for the lead. Coordinator actions arrive on nudges and on multi-recipient result sets. The second nudge after the late reply is the envelope that binds the `completed` resolve.
- The lead stays idle for the whole drive, so every nudge to it is acknowledged on dispatch and would itself settle as `no_reply` 30 s later. The helper completes each nudge within a second of its arrival.
- The nudge arrives in the same drain as the settle. The helper waits for a nudge whose `control.coordination_id` matches the case, so the second coordination's nudge is never confused with the first's.
- The simulated agent never reads its envelopes and runs no model. The helper runs the bound operations on its behalf, so this proves the product's settle, attention and transport behavior, not agent behavior or real agent hooks.
